About CueLens

CueLens provides real-time emotional subtitles that offer autistic and neurodivergent people optional context about facial-expression and tone cues. By combining expression-aware AI with accessibility-centered design, CueLens supports clearer communication across neurotypes, languages, and cultures.

  • Real-time context

    Emotional subtitles make expression and tone cues easier to access in the moment.

  • Accessibility-led AI

    Expression-aware AI is paired with accessibility design to support neurodivergent users.

  • Culturally aware communication

    CueLens is built for clearer communication across neurotypes, languages, and cultures.
